The Mercy of Allah

Allah's mercy is greater than we can imagine. Even when we make mistakes, we should never lose hope in the mercy of Allah.

Life can sometimes feel difficult and our hearts can become tired. At those moments, turn towards Allah with sincere dua and remember that Allah knows what is in our hearts.

Never lose hope in the mercy of Allah. Keep making dua, keep remembering Him, and keep trying to do what is good.

A mistake does not have to be the end of your journey. We can learn, repent, improve ourselves, and continue moving towards what is good.

May Allah forgive our mistakes, strengthen our hearts, guide us to the straight path, and bless us with His mercy.

Ameen 🤲
